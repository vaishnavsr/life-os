# Research sources

Aim for a brief a hiring manager would recognise as informed. Spend effort in proportion to the role's seniority. Always summarise in your own words, and never paste large chunks of copyrighted reports.

## Company (all countries)
- **Listed companies**: the latest annual report, half-year or quarterly results, investor presentation and strategy day materials, from the investor relations page.
- **Private companies and start-ups**: the company website (about, careers, newsroom), recent press, funding announcements (Crunchbase or similar, if reachable) and founder interviews.
- **Government and not-for-profit**: the annual report, strategic plan, budget papers and the minister's or board's priorities.
- **Signals worth noting**: recent leadership changes, restructures, acquisitions, product launches, and anything the job ad hints at ("newly created role", "transformation").

## Industry regulators and bodies, by country
Use `settings.locale.country` and the employer's industry. These are starting points; search for current equivalents.

**Australia**
- Energy: AEMO (ISP, IASR, ESOO), AER, AEMC, Clean Energy Regulator.
- Banking and finance: APRA, RBA, ASIC, ACCC.
- Insurance and health insurance: APRA, the Department of Health, the Private Health Insurance Ombudsman.
- Telecommunications: ACMA and ACCC. Health: AIHW and state health departments. Infrastructure: Infrastructure Australia. Data: ABS.

**New Zealand**
- Electricity Authority, Commerce Commission, Reserve Bank of NZ, FMA, Stats NZ.

**United Kingdom**
- Ofgem, FCA, PRA and the Bank of England, CMA, Ofcom, ONS. Health: NHS England.

**United States**
- Energy: FERC, EIA, state PUCs.
- Finance: SEC, Federal Reserve, OCC, CFPB, FDIC.
- Also FCC, FTC, CMS (health) and BLS.

**Canada**
- OSFI, Bank of Canada, CRTC, Canada Energy Regulator, Statistics Canada.

**Singapore**
- MAS, EMA, IMDA, SingStat.

**India**
- RBI, SEBI, TRAI, CERC, MoSPI.

Add the user's own preferred sources from `settings.research.extra_sources`.

## Library and paid databases
Use these only when `settings.research.library_database.enabled` is true and browser tools are available.
- Use the user's existing signed-in browser session and the access route recorded in settings, e.g. a state library's guide page with an "Access database" link through the library's proxy.
- Company profile pages can usually be read as text. Some industry reports render visually and need zoomed screenshots.
- Never type a password. If the user is signed out, mark it "<database> pending" and carry on.
- Respect the library's terms, which are usually personal research only: summarise, and never download or redistribute reports.
