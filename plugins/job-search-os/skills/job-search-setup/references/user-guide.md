# How to use Job Search OS (content for the user's Notion "How to use" page)

Personalise the bracketed parts. Write it in the user's spelling.

---

**Your job search runs on three things:** an experience library (My Experiences), a job pipeline (Job Pipeline), and Claude doing the busywork in the background.

## Save a job you like (10 seconds, from your phone)
1. In the [Seek / LinkedIn / Indeed] app, open the job and tap **Share → Notion**.
2. Choose the **Job Pipeline** database and set **STATUS** to **00 - INBOX**.
3. For LinkedIn jobs, also paste the job description into the page, because LinkedIn pages need a login to read.

At the next run ([schedule]), Claude researches the company and industry, writes a brief, drafts a tailored resume and cover letter, attaches them, and moves the job to **01 - DRAFT**.

## Review and apply
Open the job in Notion, read the brief and the drafts, and ask Claude for changes ("make the cover letter warmer", "lead with the pricing work"). Then apply yourself and move the job to **02 - SUBMITTED**. Claude never submits applications for you.

## Statuses update themselves
Claude reads your email ([times]) and moves jobs along when you get a screening call, interview invite, case study, offer or rejection. Each change is logged in the job's NOTES with the email that triggered it. It never sends, replies to or deletes email, and it never marks a job Accepted; that one is yours.

## Grow your story library
Good applications come from good stories. Any time, open Claude and say **"let's add a story"**, then talk through something you did. Claude asks follow-up questions (numbers, your role, what happened next) and saves it to My Experiences. Short on time? Record a voice memo, paste the transcript into a new My Experiences page, and Claude will tidy it up and list the questions it still needs answered. Say **"finish my draft stories"** when you have five minutes.

## Useful things to say
- "process my inbox": draft everything in 00 - INBOX now
- "check my email for job updates"
- "let's add a story" / "finish my draft stories"
- "add [library database] research to the [company] job" (when you're at your computer and signed in)
- "change my Job Search OS settings"

## Settings
Your preferences (currency, page size, writing rules, schedule) live on the **Job Search OS – Settings** page. You can edit them, or ask Claude to.
